Houston-rooted care with decades of experience

Primetime Home Health Services is based in southwest Houston and has served area families for more than 25 years. Our team focuses on non-medical personal assistance services that help eligible clients complete daily routines safely and consistently in their own homes.

Services available to eligible Houston clients

  • Attendant care services
  • Activities of Daily Living assistance
  • Personal care
  • Respite care
  • Medication reminders
  • Protective supervision when authorized through an applicable program

Working with Houston families and health plans

Coverage depends on eligibility, authorization and plan participation. Primetime works with multiple Texas Medicaid managed-care programs and can help families identify the correct next step after they receive or request authorization.

Why local experience matters

A Houston-based care team understands the size of the metro area, the importance of dependable scheduling, and the need to coordinate services around families’ daily lives. Our office provides a local point of contact for service questions, authorizations and caregiver support.

Houston home care FAQs

What parts of Houston do you serve?

We serve eligible clients across Greater Houston. Availability depends on ZIP code, service needs, payer and caregiver coverage.

Do you accept Medicaid plans?

Primetime participates with multiple Texas Medicaid health plans. Contact us with your plan name so we can confirm current participation.

Can a family caregiver be involved?

Some programs allow eligible family members or trusted individuals to participate as attendants, subject to program rules and enrollment requirements.
