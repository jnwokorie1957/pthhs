PRIMETIME PRODUCT DIRECTION: VISIT MAINTENANCE FIRST
Owner direction recorded November 5, 2026, America/Chicago.
Status: accepted product requirements and implementation order; not a release,
staff-account grant, source-write activation or started pilot.

PRIMARY OUTCOME
Reduce the office time spent maintaining visits. Primetime should become the
daily operating screen for the owner, office staff, supervisors and office
manager. They should review the office overview, real caregiver call-ins and
call-outs, attempts, source exceptions, applicable reason codes and confirmation
results, and perform supported maintenance without leaving Primetime.
HHA remains the current underlying record system during this phase.

The owner explicitly confirmed: use ACTUAL CAREGIVER CALLS ONLY. The agent should
process and, when eventually authorized, confirm genuine events supplied by
caregivers. Keep original event timestamps and provenance intact. A missing call
must not be manufactured from the schedule. A recorded visit time, raw call
attempt, accepted call-to-visit link and confirmed visit are separate evidence.

Maintenance should become assisted and then selectively automated. Repetitive
cases with an approved deterministic correction rule are candidates. Larger,
ambiguous, unsupported or failed cases go to staff with a useful explanation.
The owner also wants reports explaining which maintenance was completed and why.
The longer-term objective is an owned scheduling platform after the management
dashboard and maintenance workflow have proved reliable in daily operation.

WORKSPACE AND ROLE DESIGN (PROPOSED; ACCESS NOT ACTIVATED)
One shared Visit Maintenance workspace should connect Overview, source visits,
EVV attempts, review actions, confirmations and the office follow-up report.
Role-specific views use the same scoped records, not disconnected dashboards.

Administrator: full authorized office overview; configure team access, permitted
actions, correction policies, pilot membership and automation rollout controls.
Office staff: assigned office/client work queue, source events, proposed changes,
approved maintenance actions and a clear handoff for unresolved cases.
Supervisor: source evidence and escalated cases, review decisions and supported
approvals within explicitly assigned authority.
Office manager: office/team overview, assignments, workload, completed-action and
unresolved-case reports, escalation ownership and permitted review actions.

Every account needs its own identity, office/client scope, effective membership
and action permissions. An office role must not inherit administrator access to
HHA credentials, owner settings or integration grants. The current deployed app
still admits administrators; existing backend role names do not prove restricted
staff login, assignments or permissions are finished.

VISIT MAINTENANCE SCREEN
1. Office/day and assigned-client filters, truthful source freshness and coverage.
2. Schedule, assigned caregiver and supplied recorded/EVV start and end values.
3. Actual call attempts with source IDs, timestamps, types, statuses and available
   failure reasons; linked, suggested, rejected and nonmatching states stay
   distinct where the source provides a verified mapping.
4. Exact visit/event relationship evidence; same patient/caregiver/day alone is
   insufficient to silently link an event to one of several visits.
5. Applicable source reason/action catalog and agency-approved policy. Display
   raw codes and meanings; suggestions are not an applied reason or confirmation.
6. Before/proposed values, original call evidence, explanation and required note.
7. Submit through a supported HHA action interface, then independently re-read
   and show the actual HHA result. Local follow-up resolution is a separate action.
8. Assigned next step or verified action receipt; no successful banner on a draft,
   HTTP transport success alone, unresolved write result or local note save.

DELIVERY ORDER AND PILOT
A. Consolidate the human Visit Maintenance workbench and office reports; connect
   the missing authoritative source data and supported action paths.
B. Add individual scoped staff/supervisor/manager access and server-side action
   permissions. Test denied cross-office/client access and revoked membership.
C. Run a shadow pilot for a small selected department, approximately 25 clients.
   The actual department, private client list, dates and staff accounts have not
   been selected. Keep membership in protected runtime storage, never Git.
   Agent proposes case classifications/actions; staff compare them with actual
   maintenance. Measure time saved, proposal accuracy, rework and escalation load.
D. Introduce staff-approved submissions with source readback and immutable audit.
   This is an implementation stage, not a new owner decision that every future
   automated action must permanently require approval.
E. Allow limited automatic processing only for explicitly approved, tested rules
   within the pilot scope. Original caregiver events, exact identities, authority,
   current evidence and required reason/action mappings must all be established.
   Model confidence alone does not authorize a correction. No guessed time,
   invented call, unsupported round-off or clinical/payroll/billing decision.
   Conflicts, multiple matches, missing evidence, unknown results and unsupported
   adjustments stop automatic execution and become assigned staff cases.
F. Expand only against owner-agreed pilot results; provide immediate pause and
   rule disablement. Missing acceptance thresholds must not be invented.
G. Develop the owned scheduling platform after maintenance is reliable. Preserve
   visit/event identities and history for that later transition. Do not silently
   introduce a second authoritative scheduler or duplicate live schedules now.

STAFF REPORTS / ACTION HISTORY
Initially use protected in-app reports and assigned work queues. Delivery to a
separate channel needs named recipients/channel configuration; no communication
has been sent by recording this direction.

Resolved: visit and original event references, issue, approved rule/version,
before/proposed/actual changes, reason/action/note, actor or agent version, human
approver where applicable, HHA response and independently verified readback.
Explain: "Resolved this maintenance because [evidence and approved rule]."
Unresolved: exact obstacle, missing/conflicting evidence, attempted actions and
their known outcomes, assigned staff member/role and the next necessary action.
Explain: "Could not resolve because [specific reason]; staff must [next step]."
Retain pending/unknown outcomes separately from confirmed success or failure.
Reports contain private operational evidence and require the same scoped access.

ACCEPTANCE / CURRENT LIMITS
Require genuine-source test cases for linked and unlinked attempts, duplicate
calls, multiple same-day visits, absent punches, source conflicts, changed staff
scope, revoked access, reason applicability, submit-once behavior and unknown
outcome reconciliation. Verify real HHA persistence before calling any case
confirmed. A local review, draft, suggestion or report cannot be counted as an
HHA correction. No new write, read grant, staff account, pilot or automation was
enabled by this requirements update. Existing source/clinical decisions remain
open unless independently accepted. This direction does not create or restart a
Codex goal or change recurring tasks.
