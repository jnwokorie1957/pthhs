SCHEDULE-GATE-001 - STOP WHEN EVV EXCEEDS SCHEDULED HOURS
Version: 2. Explicit owner instruction and first candidate-overage stop November 6.

The owner directed paying attention to scheduled hours and stopping to work
together whenever EVV is greater than the scheduled hours.

1. Inspect the selected visit's scheduled hours and actual EVV evidence before
   linking a call, changing a field or submitting. Keep scheduled, original
   EVV and adjusted Bill Time values separate.
2. If EVV exceeds the schedule, stop on that visit and wait for the owner. Do
   not continue other dates, apply a reason/action, link or save that case while
   waiting. Preserve the precise issue in protected evidence and explain it
   without publishing identities or raw timestamps.
3. An adjusted bill-clock span that fits the schedule does not remove an EVV
   overage. Do not round, cap, shift or invent punches to make the comparison
   fit. The earlier approved one-hour example preceded this stop instruction
   and is not permission to handle a new overage automatically.
4. Inspect genuine candidate calls before pairing them. If the available pair
   would exceed the schedule, stop before linking it. Missing endpoints or
   unclear units/date coverage make the comparison unknown, not zero; obtain
   clarification before a source write when the gate cannot be assessed.
5. If the inspected call searches return No Records, leave the visit unchanged
   and unresolved, then continue only within the requested past-day review.
   Do not mark a missing-call visit confirmed merely to clear its queue.
