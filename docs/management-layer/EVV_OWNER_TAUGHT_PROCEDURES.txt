2. Distinguish the scheduled span from the separate green EVV span. The owner

4. Click that day's V or its EVV time link, then Visit Info. Verify the visit

8. Missing calls on successive days is an owner-taught investigation heuristic:
   the caregiver may not have called in their time yet. Check each requested
