## Implementation rules

- Keep canonical clean routes and descriptive links. Repair demonstrated faults
  before changing working public copy for speculative ranking gains.
- Answer actual visitor questions with verified information and a clear next
  step. Visible FAQs can help readers; Google's FAQ rich-result feature ended
  in May 2026. [Google updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates)
- Retain the selective Organization/WebSite/WebPage, Service and BreadcrumbList
  contracts. Do not invent local branches or ratings. Business-controlled
  reviews, including widgets, are ineligible for Organization/LocalBusiness
  review stars. [Review guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/review-snippet)
- Avoid word-count quotas, cosmetic date changes and locality substitutions.
  [People-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content)
- Apply the [measured keyword method](PTHHS_STRIKING_DISTANCE_KEYWORDS.md).
  Impressions or estimated competitor ranks do not establish qualified leads,
  PTHHS rankings or a need for another landing page.

Record user purpose, page, evidence, exact scope and acceptance for each change.
Verify claims, metadata, navigation, accessibility, assets, private protections
and production output through the existing release workflow.

The footer repair addresses two navigation gaps. Blog/about-style landing
discoverability and the remaining location inventory need page-purpose review.
Sitemap warning details/reprocessing and optional CrUX configuration remain
owner actions. No growth is established; private HHA/admin acceptance remains
in the management checklists.
