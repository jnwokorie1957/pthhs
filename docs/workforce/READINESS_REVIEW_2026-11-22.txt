Finish: TMHP interface/enrollment, agency credentials/authority, explicit service-date request/response mapping, member identity validation, freshness/renewal handling, outage behavior and live acceptance. MEDIUM PRIORITY / LATER-PHASE COMPLETION

Finish: representative payer-format acceptance, field confidence/ambiguity handling, source/evidence review and, if wanted, an agency-approved PHI-safe AI service/processing arrangement. Do not silently send patient documents to an unapproved model or treat extracted dates as independently verified coverage.

6. Service-continuation and department reminder automation remain partly operational/manual.
Evidence: source/workforce/workforce-office-tools.js; functions/src/workforce/staffOperations.ts (renewal, expirationSummary, memberCoverage).
Portal alerts have full-office totals and 15/30-day groups; client coverage scans disclose limits and unlinked records. Continuing services are explicitly staff-confirmed, not derived from an accepted live schedule/eligibility feed. Authorization reminder emails and dedicated departmental ownership are not enabled or designated. A recorded request/accepted email is not verified delivery.
Finish: assign the department/staff/view grants, approved recipients and escalation/hold policy; accept the exact service/schedule evidence if automatic continuation detection is required; add a durable reminder outbox with safe uncertain-send handling before enabling email. Preserve human review for holds and case-manager contact.

7. Reusable signatures remain local appearances in a witnessed in-person workflow.
Evidence: public/assets/workforce-signature.js; source/workforce/workforce-field-visits.js; docs/workforce/MEDIUM_FOLLOW_UP_2026-11-13.md.
They are encrypted for the same browser/account/signer context. They do not follow a client to another laptop/browser or prove remote signer identity. This walkthrough actually encountered a client with no local saved appearance. It is not a complete remote-signing platform.
Finish: agency-approved signer/representative identity, consent, device sharing, revocation/retention and authenticated remote-invitation model if cross-device/remote signing is needed. Do not fix this merely by putting signature images in an openly reusable database collection.

8. Packet preparation is a sequence of durable draft writes, not one atomic operation.
Evidence: source/workforce/workforce-form-workflow.js; functions/src/workforce/admissionPackets.ts.
Packet membership is now durable and completion is server-derived; reopening and combined export are supported. If a tab closes while individual drafts are being prepared, some unsigned drafts can exist before the packet group is saved. Group-save retry is idempotent, but a complete resumable server-side preparation outbox is not implemented. Nothing in that partial state becomes fully signed or exportable.
Finish: durable preparation intent/outbox with stable draft IDs and immutable template versions if unattended large-packet preparation/resume is required. Keep incomplete drafts clearly identified and avoid duplicate packet starts.

PRIVACY / VERIFICATION BOUNDARIES

- Stored authorization binaries and clinical-form objects use AES-256-GCM envelope encryption with Cloud KMS wrapping, hashes, pinned generations and server-only access. Structured database fields retain the platform's encryption at rest; this is not field-level application encryption of every metadata value.
- A preview that an authorized person can read cannot prevent operating-system screenshots or copying displayed information. Portal export endpoints and buttons can enforce completion; they are not DRM.
- Authorization document viewers now need an explicit separate permission and recent password confirmation. No production grant was silently expanded; designated viewers/recipients are still an agency decision.
- TMHP states that prior authorization is not a payment guarantee and eligibility must be checked for the date of service: https://www.tmhp.com/sites/default/files/microsites/provider-manuals/tmppm/html/TMPPM/1_05_Prior_Authorization/1_05_Prior_Authorization.htm
- Test counts, CI receipts, exact branch/release state and any live checks must be reported with the release. Synthetic tests do not demonstrate real signatures, real payer interpretation, TMHP eligibility or actual HHA writes. No unsupported vendor capabilities or production facts were fabricated in this review.
