# HHA connections and portal placement

Generated from the Operations capability inventory by `node scripts/build-hha-portal-inventory.mjs`. All 222 captured methods are accounted for. Inventory presence is not proof of vendor entitlement or live acceptance.

Five source reads now have caregiver views; four also have staff caregiver views. Profiles, files, staff roster synchronization and staff schedules retain their existing connections. Pay-rate views are self-only; no staff payroll permission is inferred from account support. Patient, clinical, billing, payroll and source writes remain behind the separate Operations authorization and existing activation gates. No vendor method, deployment data gate, owner setting or role grant is enabled by this change.

Each new request derives identity on the server, proves office membership before and after the read, checks current settings and releases an explicit field list. Medical results, internal notes, patient identifiers and banking details are excluded. Reads are on demand and share the existing vendor budget; no background polling was added. Partial results and unavailable connections remain distinct from empty results.

## Acceptance still required

- An authorized active caregiver should check all enabled views in production.
- Authorized staff should check a caregiver in their office, an inaccessible office and a changed/revoked grant.
- Source methods that are not owner-enabled remain pending; vendor/owner prerequisites must be verified before enabling them.
- Unimplemented, adapter-only and gated mutation methods in the table are remaining work in their own domains. This release does not claim all 222 vendor workflows are implemented.
