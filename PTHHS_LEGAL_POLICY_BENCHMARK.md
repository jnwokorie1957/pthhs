# PTHHS Website Privacy & Terms Benchmark

Research date: September 9, 2026

Purpose: benchmark public website privacy policies and website terms used by Houston-area home-care providers and large U.S. home-care/home-health operators, then synthesize the useful patterns for Primetime Home Health Services, Inc. (PTHHS). This is a drafting and product-compliance benchmark, not legal advice.

## Houston-area competitors reviewed

1. **At Your Side Home Care — Houston** — Privacy Policy: https://www.houston-homecare.com/privacy-policy/
   - Separates ordinary website data from care records.
   - Discloses form/contact data, technical data, cookies, service-provider sharing, security, third-party links and SMS opt-out mechanics.
   - States mobile information is not sold/shared for third-party marketing.
2. **Candor Living Home Care Services — Houston** — Terms and Conditions: https://candorlivinghcs.com/terms-and-conditions/
   - Particularly strong plain-English distinction between website terms and the separate written care-services agreement.
   - Prominent emergency/911 notice and no-care-relationship-by-website language.
3. **Allied Home Health — Houston** — Website Terms: https://alliedhomehealthhouston.com/website-terms/
   - Uses Texas governing law and standard website-use, third-party-link and amendment clauses.
4. **Chosen Path Home Care — Houston** — Terms/Privacy: https://chosenpathhomecare.com/terms-of-service-and-privacy-policy/
   - Covers truthful submissions, website/app usage, contact data and changes to terms.
5. **At Your Side / ComForCare Houston-area offices** — https://atyoursidehomecare.com/privacy-policy/
   - More detailed information-security/governance and vendor obligations; useful as a benchmark for security wording without promising absolute security.

## Nationwide / large operators reviewed

1. **Right at Home** — Privacy Policy: https://www.rightathome.net/privacy ; Terms of Use: https://www.rightathome.net/privacy/terms-of-use
   - Clearly distinguishes website information from HIPAA-regulated information.
   - Enumerates user-provided data, health information, device/IP/cookie/analytics data and third-party tracking.
   - Terms contain medical-information disclaimer, IP/acceptable-use provisions, third-party links and arbitration/class-action provisions.
2. **Visiting Angels** — Privacy Policy: https://www.visitingangels.com/southcentral/privacy-policy ; Terms: https://www.visitingangels.com/southjax/terms-and-conditions
   - Explicitly says website Privacy Notice is not the same thing as the HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices.
   - Standard website-use, age, privacy, change-of-terms and service-information disclaimers.
3. **Addus HomeCare** — Privacy Policy: https://addus.com/privacy-policy/ ; Terms: https://addus.com/terms-of-use/
   - Strong separation between website personal information and medical information governed by a HIPAA notice.
   - Covers passive collection, cookies, retention, security limits, links and SMS privacy risks.
   - Terms include IP, U.S.-audience, communications, governing law and arbitration language.
4. **Aveanna Healthcare** — Privacy Policy: https://www.aveanna.com/privacypolicy/ ; HIPAA Notice: https://www.aveanna.com/HIPAANotice.html
   - Explicit HIPAA cross-reference, service-provider restrictions, no-sale statement, security limitations, children/COPPA treatment and privacy-right request channels.
5. **Comfort Keepers** — Privacy Policy: https://www.comfortkeepers.com/offices/rhode-island/lincoln/privacy-policy/ ; HIPAA Notice: https://www.comfortkeepers.com/hipaa-notice-of-privacy-practices/
   - Separates general personal-data practices from PHI/HIPAA obligations and sets out data-protection/security concepts.
6. **BrightStar Care** — representative local Privacy Policy: https://www.brightstarcare.com/locations/brookville/privacy-policy/
   - Tells users not to place health or sensitive information into ordinary web forms; covers affiliates, service providers, business transfers, legal process and marketing uses.
7. **Home Instead** — legal/privacy resources and local Houston pages: https://www.homeinstead.com/contact-us/ and https://www.homeinstead.com/home-care/usa/tx/houston/541/
   - Separates Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, SMS terms and Client Notice of Privacy Practices.
   - Uses explicit SMS consent language, opt-out mechanisms and warnings about privacy risks of electronic communications.

## Drafting decisions for PTHHS

### Included
- Scope limited to PTHHS website/digital interactions, with a clear HIPAA/NPP carve-out.
- Contact/intake, caregiver/job inquiry, payer/eligibility inquiry and technical/cookie data categories.
- Purpose-limited use: respond to inquiries, coordinate next steps, operate/security/analytics, recruitment and legal compliance.
- Vendor/service-provider, payer/referral-partner (when authorized/necessary), legal/safety and business-transfer disclosures.
- No sale of personal information for money; no sharing of mobile opt-in/consent data for third-party marketing.
- Reasonable security language without an impossible guarantee.
- Retention only as reasonably necessary/legal.
- Children: site directed to adults/parents/guardians even though PTHHS may serve minors as clients.
- State privacy-right request language framed as applicable-law rights rather than overclaiming a particular statute's applicability.
- Plain-language emergency and no-medical-advice disclaimers.
- Website inquiry does not create a caregiver/client relationship or guarantee eligibility, authorization, staffing, payer coverage or start date.
- Texas law / Harris County venue for ordinary website disputes.

### Deliberately not imported
- **Mandatory arbitration / class-action waiver.** Several large operators use it, but it materially changes visitor rights and should not be inserted into PTHHS terms without Texas counsel and a deliberate business decision.
- **Broad perpetual license to user-generated content.** PTHHS does not appear to run a public user-content platform, so this would be unnecessary.
- **Franchise-specific disclaimers.** PTHHS is presented as one Houston-based agency, not a national franchise network.
- **Clinical-treatment promises.** The current PTHHS site positions the agency primarily around non-medical personal assistance services.

## Items for counsel / operations to confirm before treating as final legal copy

1. Whether PTHHS is a HIPAA Covered Entity and/or Business Associate for each service line and payer arrangement, and the exact current Notice of Privacy Practices that should be linked or published separately.
2. Whether any website form currently sends or stores PHI and, if so, whether every vendor/path is configured under appropriate HIPAA safeguards/BAAs.
3. Exact analytics, advertising pixels, cookie-consent tooling and form vendors in production.
4. Whether PTHHS sends automated or promotional SMS. If yes, publish a separate SMS Terms page and ensure form-level TCPA consent language matches actual messaging practices.
5. Confirm the preferred privacy-contact role/address and whether `pas@pthhs.net` should receive privacy-right requests.
6. Confirm whether a Texas-specific consumer privacy request/appeal workflow is required for PTHHS's non-HIPAA data under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act or other applicable state laws.
