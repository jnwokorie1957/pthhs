# PTHHS Remaining Human or External-System Blockers

**Updated:** September 11, 2026

## Forms and analytics

The static site currently publishes no contact, appointment, or career form. Items 36–47 cannot be completed safely without an approved server-side destination, accountable recipient/escalation path, retention/privacy rules, and—if events are required—an analytics property identifier and consent decision. The site continues to provide phone, email, office, after-hours, privacy, and emergency guidance without presenting a form that could lose or expose a lead.

## Browser and assistive-technology QA

The committed responsive harness covers the required 320, 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixel widths, but Chromium installation repeatedly timed out in the current execution environment. Browser zoom/reflow, automated browser accessibility, and manual keyboard/screen-reader signoff therefore remain open. Static accessibility, landmark, contrast, image, link, and responsive CSS gates remain active.

## Production and search systems

The configured HSTS policy and the changes in this branch cannot be verified on production until they are deployed. A post-deployment production crawl, redirect/header check, Search Console canonical-property verification, sitemap submission, Google Business Profile account check, and analytics/dashboard configuration require the applicable deployment or authenticated account access.
