# PTHHS Approved Public Terminology

**Effective:** September 10, 2026  
**Owner:** PTHHS administrator  
**Applies to:** Website, social media, ads, directory listings, handouts, scripts, and AI-generated copy

## Approved positioning

- Primetime Home Health Services, Inc. is a Houston-based provider of **non-medical personal assistance services (PAS)**.
- Approved service terms include personal assistance, attendant care, personal care, respite care, activities of daily living (ADL) support, authorized protective supervision, and non-medical medication reminders.
- Eligibility, covered tasks, authorized hours, and network status are determined by the applicable program or payer and can change.
- Primetime may explain its agency onboarding process after eligibility and authorization are confirmed.

## Required distinctions

- “Home care” may describe non-medical everyday support when the same page makes the non-medical scope clear.
- “Home health” may appear in the legal business name. As a generic service category, it must be used only to explain that skilled home health is different from PTHHS's non-medical PAS.
- Medication reminders are prompts only. Attendants do not select, prepare, measure, administer, change, or clinically evaluate medication.
- PTHHS does not diagnose conditions or provide skilled nursing, medical treatment, therapy, wound care, or clinical monitoring through the services described on the website.

## Prohibited unless documented and approved

- Home health agency, skilled home health, skilled nursing, nurse-provided care, therapy, treatment, wound care, medication administration, clinical monitoring, or specialized medical attention as descriptions of PTHHS services.
- Claims that PTHHS accepts, contracts with, participates in, or is in-network with a named payer without current evidence in the payer register.
- Awards, rankings, customer totals, satisfaction percentages, founding dates, years-in-business counts, superlatives, or guarantees without a dated source.
- Diagnosis or condition lists that suggest PTHHS diagnoses, treats, or specializes in a medical condition.
- “Home-health professional” or “home-health aide” for attendant roles unless HR has approved that exact classification.

## Safe payer language

> Eligibility, covered services, authorization, and provider-network participation can change. Contact the plan and Primetime to confirm current status before services begin.

## Claim approval rule

Before publication, any clinical, payer, credential, award, historical, or quantitative claim must have a source, effective date, owner, and re-check date in the applicable evidence register. If evidence is absent or expired, remove or soften the claim.
