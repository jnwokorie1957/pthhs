Meaningful work starts with everyday care.

Our non-medical personal assistance services support eligible seniors, adults, children and people with disabilities at home.

Compassion

Respect each person’s dignity, preferences and routines while providing thoughtful daily support.

Dependability

Communicate clearly, follow assigned schedules and provide support within the authorized service plan.

Professionalism

Respect privacy, follow agency procedures and complete the requirements for the role.

Connect with our team.

Contact our office for current openings, application instructions and role-specific requirements.

Start your career conversation

Call our office or email a brief introduction with your name, contact information and interest in personal assistance work. Our team will explain how to apply.

Call 713-977-7721Email Our Team

Please do not send Social Security numbers, identity documents, medical records or other sensitive information by email. Contacting us starts an inquiry; it does not submit a completed employment application.

What happens next?

  1. Ask about current opportunities and available schedules.
  2. Receive the appropriate application instructions from our team.
  3. Complete the required screening and onboarding steps for the role.

Availability, duties, schedules and employment requirements are confirmed during the hiring process.
