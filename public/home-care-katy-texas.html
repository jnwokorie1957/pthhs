Katy Home Care Services | Primetime Home Health Services

Primetime Home Health Services, Inc. proudly serves families in Katy and the greater West Houston area with professional, compassionate in-home care. Our mission is to deliver reliable support that enables seniors, veterans, and families to remain safe and independent in their own homes.

Our Commitment to Katy: Compassionate Care, Every Day

Primetime Home Health Services eliminates the barriers that prevent Katy families from accessing high-quality home care. We maintain a team of background-checked, trained caregivers who specialize in personal assistance, medication management, respite care, and dementia support. Our caregivers are CPR-certified and understand the unique needs of Katy’s growing communities.
