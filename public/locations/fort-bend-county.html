Fort Bend County coverage from a long-standing local agency

Primetime Home Health Services is a long-standing local agency serving Fort Bend County families. Our focus is non-medical personal assistance services (PAS) that help eligible clients complete daily routines safely in their own homes subject to the applicable program and authorization.

Services available to eligible Fort Bend County clients

  • Attendant care services
  • Activities of Daily Living assistance
  • Personal care
  • Respite care
  • Medication reminders
  • Protective supervision when authorized through an applicable program

Fort Bend County communities we serve

Primetime provides eligible PAS services throughout Fort Bend County, including Sugar Land, Missouri City, Richmond, Rosenberg, and surrounding communities. Service availability depends on ZIP code, health plan authorization, and caregiver coverage.

Working with Fort Bend County families and health plans

Coverage depends on eligibility, authorization and plan participation. Network participation can change. Primetime can help families identify the correct next step after they receive or request authorization.

Fort Bend County home care FAQs

Which parts of Fort Bend County does Primetime serve?
