Hempstead-rooted care

Primetime Home Health Services is a Houston-based agency serving Greater Houston families, including Hempstead. Our focus is non-medical personal assistance services that help eligible clients complete daily routines safely in their own homes.

Services available to eligible Hempstead clients

  • Attendant care services
  • Activities of Daily Living assistance
  • Personal care
  • Respite care
  • Medication reminders

Local Hempstead resources

Hempstead families can connect with Waller County Area Agency on Aging and local community programs. Primetime provides the non-medical PAS layer that complements clinical care available at nearby hospitals.
