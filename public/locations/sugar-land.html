Personal assistance for Fort Bend families

Families in Sugar Land may be caring for aging parents, children with disabilities or relatives who need help staying safe and comfortable at home. Primetime provides authorized non-medical assistance designed around everyday routines and the individual’s approved needs.

Services may include

  • Attendant and personal care
  • Bathing, dressing and grooming assistance
  • Mobility, positioning and transfers
  • Meal-related support and light housekeeping tied to the care plan
  • Respite care for family caregivers
  • Medication reminders when allowed by the authorized plan

Checking coverage in Sugar Land

Service availability can vary by ZIP code, managed-care plan, program and authorization. Contact Primetime with the client’s plan and location so our team can confirm the current status.
