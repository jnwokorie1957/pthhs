Local support for Alief households

Alief is one of Houston’s most diverse communities, with multigenerational households and families balancing work, school, medical appointments and caregiving responsibilities. Primetime provides authorized, non-medical personal assistance that can make day-to-day care more manageable while helping clients remain at home.

Services for eligible clients

  • Attendant care and help with daily routines
  • Bathing, grooming, dressing and toileting assistance
  • Mobility and transfer support
  • Meal-related assistance and light housekeeping tied to the care plan
  • Respite support for family caregivers
  • Non-medical medication reminders when authorized

Medicaid and plan coordination

If a client receives services through a Texas Medicaid managed-care plan, authorization rules and available hours are determined by the applicable program. Our office can help confirm whether Primetime participates with the client’s current plan and explain the agency onboarding steps after authorization.

A Houston office close to southwest and west Houston

Our Burdine Street office gives Alief families a local agency contact rather than a distant national call center. We support scheduling, authorization questions and caregiver coordination from Houston.

Alief home care FAQs

Does Primetime serve Alief ZIP codes?

We serve eligible clients in Alief and surrounding west/southwest Houston areas, subject to service availability, authorization and payer requirements.

What type of care do you provide?

Primetime provides non-medical personal assistance services rather than skilled nursing services.

How do I start?

Contact our office with the client’s location, health plan and requested service. We will explain the appropriate next step.
