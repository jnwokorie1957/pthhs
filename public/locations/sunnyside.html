Home Care and Personal Assistance in Sunnyside

Community-focused, non-medical support for eligible Sunnyside residents who need assistance with everyday care at home.

Helping Sunnyside families support loved ones at home

Home-based personal assistance can reduce the pressure on families who are balancing work, appointments, school and caregiving. Primetime caregivers provide approved non-medical assistance with daily routines while respecting the client’s dignity, preferences and household.

Available support may include

  • Attendant care
  • Bathing, dressing and grooming support
  • Mobility and transfer assistance
  • Meal-related assistance and light housekeeping connected to care needs
  • Respite services for family caregivers
  • Medication reminders when authorized

Local guidance through the care process

Families can contact our Houston office with questions about current plan participation, service availability and agency enrollment after authorization.

Sunnyside FAQs

Do you serve Sunnyside?

Yes, Primetime serves eligible clients in Sunnyside and other Greater Houston communities, subject to payer, authorization and staffing availability.

What is personal assistance?

Personal assistance is non-medical help with everyday activities such as bathing, dressing, mobility and other approved routines.

How do I verify coverage?

Call us with the client’s health plan and ZIP code, and we can explain the current agency participation and service-area status.
