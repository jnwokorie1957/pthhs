Supporting Gulfton’s diverse families

Families in Gulfton often coordinate care across busy work schedules, school responsibilities and multiple generations. Primetime’s goal is to make authorized home-care support dependable and understandable, with a Houston office available to answer service and authorization questions.

Personal assistance services

  • Bathing, grooming and dressing assistance
  • Mobility and transfer support
  • Attendant care for approved daily tasks
  • Respite care for family caregivers
  • Meal and household assistance connected to the authorized care plan
  • Non-medical medication reminders when permitted

Understanding eligibility

Eligibility, covered services and approved hours are determined by the relevant program or health plan. Primetime can help families understand the agency’s role and confirm whether we participate with the client’s current managed-care plan.

Gulfton FAQs
