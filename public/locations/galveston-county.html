Galveston County coverage with decades of local experience

Primetime Home Health Services has served Galveston County families for more than 25 years. Our focus is non-medical personal assistance services (PAS) that help eligible clients complete daily routines safely in their own homes under Texas Medicaid programs including STAR+PLUS.

Services available to eligible Galveston County clients

  • Attendant care services
  • Activities of Daily Living assistance
  • Personal care
  • Respite care
  • Medication reminders
  • Protective supervision when authorized through an applicable program

Galveston County communities we serve

Primetime provides eligible PAS services throughout Galveston County, including Galveston, Texas City, League City, Friendswood, Dickinson, La Marque, Santa Fe, Hitchcock, Kemah, Clear Lake Shores, Crystal Beach, Port Bolivar, and surrounding communities. Service availability depends on ZIP code, health plan authorization, and caregiver coverage.

Working with Galveston County families and health plans

Coverage depends on eligibility, authorization and plan participation. Primetime works with multiple Texas Medicaid managed-care programs and can help families identify the correct next step after they receive or request authorization.

Galveston County home care FAQs

Which parts of Galveston County does Primetime serve?

We serve eligible clients across Galveston County. Availability depends on ZIP code, service needs, payer and caregiver coverage.

Do you accept Medicaid plans in Galveston County?

Primetime participates with multiple Texas Medicaid health plans. Contact us with your plan name so we can confirm current participation.

Can a family caregiver be involved?

Some programs allow eligible family members or trusted individuals to participate as attendants, subject to program rules and enrollment requirements.
