Brazoria County coverage from a long-standing local agency

Primetime Home Health Services is a long-standing local agency serving Brazoria County families. Our focus is non-medical personal assistance services (PAS) that help eligible clients complete daily routines safely in their own homes subject to the applicable program and authorization.

Services available to eligible Brazoria County clients

  • Attendant care services
  • Activities of Daily Living assistance
  • Personal care
  • Respite care
  • Medication reminders
  • Protective supervision when authorized through an applicable program

Brazoria County communities we serve

Primetime provides eligible PAS services throughout Brazoria County, including Pearland and surrounding communities. Service availability depends on ZIP code, health plan authorization, and caregiver coverage.

Working with Brazoria County families and health plans

Coverage depends on eligibility, authorization and plan participation. Network participation can change. Primetime can help families identify the correct next step after they receive or request authorization.
