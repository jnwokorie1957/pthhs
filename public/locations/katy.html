Supporting Katy families at home

As the Houston region has grown westward, more families in Katy are coordinating care for older adults, children with disabilities and relatives who need help with everyday routines. Primetime extends its Houston-based personal assistance services to eligible Katy-area clients when the service, authorization and payer fall within our coverage.

Services may include

  • Attendant care services
  • Activities of Daily Living assistance
  • Personal care and hygiene support
  • Mobility and transfer assistance
  • Respite care
  • Non-medical medication reminders when authorized

Confirming service availability in Katy

Katy spans more than one county and many ZIP codes. Call our office with the client’s address or ZIP code, health plan and requested service so we can confirm current coverage.

Katy home care FAQs

Does Primetime serve all of Katy?

Coverage depends on the exact ZIP code, payer, service authorization and caregiver availability. Contact us for a current service-area check.

Can Medicaid cover personal assistance?

Some Texas Medicaid programs cover qualifying personal assistance services. Eligibility and hours are determined by the applicable program or health plan.

How do I contact the agency?

Call 713-977-7721 or use our contact page to send your information.
