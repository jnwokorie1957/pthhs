Primetime Home Health Services is a long-standing local agency serving Greater Houston families, including Clute,. Our focus is non-medical personal assistance services that help eligible clients complete daily routines safely in their own homes.

Clute families can connect with Brazoria County Area Agency on Aging and local community programs. Primetime provides the non-medical PAS layer that complements clinical care available at nearby hospitals.
