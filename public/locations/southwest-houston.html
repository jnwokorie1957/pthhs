Our home community

Primetime’s office is located in southwest Houston on Burdine Street. That local presence gives families a direct point of contact for questions about attendant care, authorizations, eligibility, scheduling and other personal assistance needs.

Services designed for everyday life

  • Personal care and hygiene assistance
  • Activities of Daily Living support
  • Attendant care services
  • Respite care
  • Mobility and transfer assistance
  • Medication reminders when authorized

Care that fits the approved plan

Every payer and program has its own eligibility criteria. We coordinate services according to the approved authorization and do not expand care beyond the non-medical personal assistance scope.
