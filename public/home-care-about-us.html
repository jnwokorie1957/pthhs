Care Built Around You

The power of caring, every day.

Our mission is to provide empathetic and exceptional care to eligible members of our community through thoughtful support and assistance.

Dignity

We respect each person’s routines, preferences and privacy. The goal is to support daily life without taking away a person’s sense of independence.

Dependability

Clear communication, coordinated schedules and a team families can contact are central to how we approach personal assistance.

Community

We are a Houston-based agency serving eligible children, adults and seniors across Greater Houston and surrounding communities.

Personal assistance. Not skilled medical care.

Our focus is practical, non-medical help with everyday routines. Services are based on the client’s needs and the applicable service authorization.

  • Bathing, dressing, grooming and personal hygiene
  • Assistance with mobility, meals and approved daily tasks
  • Attendant care and authorized caregiver relief
  • Non-medical medication reminders
