WHO WE ARE

Primetime Home Health Services, Inc., your trusted home health agency in Houston, Texas, offers high-quality, long-term care while remaining in the familiar comfort of your own home. We know the trust value of a solid relationship. We begin to build one with you from the very start with an in-home assessment to determine your needs. It takes someone special to become a caregiver. For the best team in the business! We insist our caregivers represent our ideals of integrity, loyalty, and empathy. We make all efforts to match their qualifications, interests, and unique characteristics with your needs.

Why Choose Primetime Home Health Services, Inc.?

We conduct background screening to ensure a drug-free and alcohol-free workplace. Primetime Home Health Services, Inc. is focused on safety.

PHILOSOPHY

Primetime Home Health Services, Inc. believes that health care is a basic human right. It must be available, coordinated, and provided in a comprehensive way, combined with other human services when appropriate. Home health care is an important part of the continuously growing healthcare system, and it shall be provided in the most cost-effective way possible. Primetime Home Health Services, Inc. and our staff shall operate and provide services in compliance with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations and disclosure and ownership information. To the best of our knowledge, based on our Quality Improvement Program and professional personal practices, our services comply with professional standards and principles. We, The Professional Staff at Primetime Home Health Services, Inc., are dedicated to providing the best medical care possible to our clients. We strive to protect the integrity and dignity of our clients, assuring confidentiality and respect at all times. We look forward to caring for you. Sincerely, Primetime Home, Health Services, Inc.

MISSION STATEMENT: THE POWER OF CARING

Our mission is to provide empathetic and exceptional care to every eligible member within our community, fostering their well-being and enriching their quality of life through tailored support and assistance.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to empower all people with physical and mental limitations by raising awareness and providing quality services to eligible members of our communities. We strive to enhance well-being and accessibility for all we serve.

Our Motto

Our Motto, The Power Of Caring, is reflected in our staff and services as we strive for excellence in patient care at all levels.

Our Features and Benefits

Recuperating at home often helps clients achieve a high level of self-esteem and also gives their families a more direct role in their care. At Primetime Home Health Services, Inc., we recognize how important this can be to the process of your recovery. This is one of the reasons our team of home care professionals will work directly with your doctor to develop a home treatment plan that is just right for you.
