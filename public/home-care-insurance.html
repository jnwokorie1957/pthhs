Getting Started

How we verify your coverage

You do not need to understand every Medicaid rule before calling us. Start with the information you already have.

01

Tell us your plan

Share the member's health plan, ZIP code and the type of daily assistance being requested.

02

Confirm eligibility and authorization

We review the information needed for agency onboarding and help clarify the authorization process.

03

Coordinate care

Once required eligibility and authorization are in place, our team can coordinate the next steps for services.

Need Help?

Not sure which plan you have?

If you have a Medicaid card or managed-care plan card, call us with the plan name and member information. We can tell you what Primetime needs to determine the next step.

  • Plan participation check
  • Service-area confirmation
  • Authorization guidance
  • Agency onboarding information
