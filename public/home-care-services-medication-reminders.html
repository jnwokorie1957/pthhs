Medication Reminders

Support to help eligible clients stay on schedule with prescribed medications while living at home.

Check EligibilityCall 713-977-7721

What medication reminders include

  • Verbal or visual prompts at scheduled times
  • Assistance with medication organization (when authorized)
  • Documentation of medication taken
  • Communication with family or care team about missed doses
  • Coordination with pharmacy or prescriber when needed

Important note

Medication reminders are non-medical support. Attendants do not administer medications, inject medications, or make clinical decisions about medications. All clinical medication management remains the responsibility of the client, family, or licensed healthcare providers.

Where we provide medication reminders

Primetime serves eligible clients across our 10-county coverage area: Brazoria, Chambers, Fort Bend, Galveston, Harris, Jefferson, Montgomery, Waller, Walker, and Wharton counties.
