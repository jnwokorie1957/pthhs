Personal Care Services

Support with daily living activities for eligible clients who want to remain safe and comfortable at home.

Check EligibilityCall 713-977-7721

What personal care includes

  • Assistance with bathing, grooming, and dressing
  • Help with toileting and incontinence care
  • Mobility assistance and transfers
  • Meal preparation and feeding assistance
  • Light housekeeping related to care
  • Companionship and safety supervision

Who can receive personal care

Eligible clients under Texas Medicaid programs (including STAR+PLUS) who have been assessed and authorized for personal care services. A service plan is developed based on the client's needs and the health plan's authorization.

Where we provide personal care

Primetime serves eligible clients across our 10-county coverage area: Brazoria, Chambers, Fort Bend, Galveston, Harris, Jefferson, Montgomery, Waller, Walker, and Wharton counties.
