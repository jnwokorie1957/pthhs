Activities of Daily Living (ADL) Assistance

Help with essential daily tasks so eligible clients can live safely and independently at home.

Check EligibilityCall 713-977-7721

What ADL assistance covers

  • Bathing and showering
  • Dressing and undressing
  • Grooming and oral hygiene
  • Toileting and incontinence care
  • Transferring and mobility support
  • Eating and meal assistance
  • Medication reminders (when authorized)

Who qualifies for ADL assistance

Clients who have been assessed by their health plan and authorized for ADL support under Texas Medicaid programs. The specific tasks and hours are determined by the client's service plan.

Where we provide ADL assistance

Primetime serves eligible clients across our 10-county coverage area: Brazoria, Chambers, Fort Bend, Galveston, Harris, Jefferson, Montgomery, Waller, Walker, and Wharton counties.
