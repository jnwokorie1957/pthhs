Personalized help with essential self-care

When a client needs support with hygiene or everyday routines, reliable personal care can make it easier to remain at home. Our caregivers follow the authorized plan of care and assist only with services permitted under the client’s program.

Personal care may include

  • Bathing and grooming assistance
  • Dressing and undressing support
  • Oral hygiene and skin-care routines
  • Toileting and personal cleanliness
  • Mobility, transferring and positioning assistance
  • Feeding support and light meal preparation
  • Companionship and encouragement during daily routines

Personal Care Services for Medicaid-eligible children

Texas Medicaid programs may provide personal care services for eligible children under age 21 who need assistance with activities of daily living. Families should use the current state or health-plan process to request an assessment and determine eligibility. Our office can explain how Primetime participates once services are authorized.

Program rules, phone numbers, eligibility criteria and authorization procedures can change. Contact the appropriate Texas Medicaid program or your managed care organization for the current official process.

Care built around dignity

Personal care is intimate. Our goal is to provide dependable assistance while respecting preferences, routines and privacy. We work to match clients with caregivers who can support the approved needs of the household professionally and consistently.

Frequently asked questions

Who can receive personal care services?

Eligibility depends on the payer or Medicaid program, functional needs and authorization. Primetime serves eligible children, adults and seniors.

Can care be provided at home?

Yes. These services are designed to support clients in the familiar environment of their home.

Do caregivers administer medication?

No. Primetime provides non-medical personal assistance. Medication reminders may be provided when permitted by the service plan, but caregivers do not independently administer medication.

Can personal care be combined with other assistance?

Depending on authorization, personal care can be coordinated with other approved daily-living and attendant services.
