Everyday support that protects dignity and independence

Activities of Daily Living are the basic self-care tasks people complete each day. Aging, disability, illness or mobility limitations can make those routines harder. Primetime caregivers provide hands-on, non-medical assistance based on the client’s authorized care plan and individual needs.

ADL support may include

  • Bathing, grooming and personal hygiene assistance
  • Dressing and clothing support
  • Toileting and incontinence-related personal care
  • Mobility, positioning and transfer assistance
  • Feeding assistance and light meal preparation
  • Light housekeeping and laundry connected to the care plan
  • Medication reminders when included in the authorized service plan
Important: Primetime Home Health Services provides non-medical personal assistance services. Caregivers do not independently diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or administer medications.

Who may benefit from ADL assistance?

ADL support can help older adults, children and adults with disabilities, people with mobility limitations, and families who need dependable assistance with daily routines. Eligibility and covered hours depend on the program, payer and authorization.

How care begins

Contact our Houston office to discuss the type of assistance needed. Our team can review the services we provide, explain the next steps for the applicable program, and coordinate care after eligibility and authorization requirements are met.

Frequently asked questions

What does ADL mean?

ADL stands for Activities of Daily Living, including core routines such as bathing, dressing, eating, toileting, grooming and moving safely around the home.

Can ADL services be customized?

Yes. Services are based on the client’s needs, program requirements and authorized care plan.

Do you serve people with disabilities?

Yes. Primetime supports eligible children, adults and seniors who need assistance with daily living tasks.

Where are services available?

We serve eligible clients throughout Greater Houston and surrounding communities within our service area.

Need help with everyday care at home?

Talk with our team about eligibility, authorization and the support that may be available for your family.
