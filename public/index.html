Questions Families Ask

Houston home care FAQs

Clear answers about personal assistance, Medicaid-supported care and getting started.

What is the difference between home care and home health care?

Home care generally refers to non-medical support with everyday routines, while skilled home health services involve clinical care provided by licensed healthcare professionals. Primetime focuses on non-medical personal assistance services.

What does Medicaid home care cover in Texas?

Coverage depends on the specific Medicaid program, health plan, functional needs and authorization. Approved services may include personal assistance with daily activities. The payer determines eligibility and authorized hours.

Can a family member become a paid caregiver?

Certain Texas programs may allow eligible family members or trusted individuals to serve as attendants, subject to program restrictions, enrollment and employment requirements. Contact us to discuss the applicable program.

Which Medicaid plans does Primetime work with?

Primetime works with UnitedHealthcare, Molina Healthcare, Community Health Choice, Wellpoint, Texas Children’s Health Plan and Traditional Texas Medicaid for applicable services. Participation can change; confirm current status with the plan and Primetime.

How quickly can services begin?

Timing depends on eligibility, authorization, required onboarding and caregiver availability. Our team can explain the current process once we have the client’s information.

What areas of Houston do you serve?

We serve eligible clients throughout Greater Houston and surrounding communities in our current service area. Use our Areas We Serve page or call to confirm a ZIP code.

Start with a local Houston team. Tell us where you live, which plan you use and the kind of assistance you need.
