Help with the routines that make home feel like home.

When included in the authorized service plan, everyday assistance may also include:

Companionship

Conversation and considerate support during approved care visits.

Laundry

Help with washing, drying, folding and putting away the client’s clothing.

Light housekeeping

Assistance with approved light household tasks in the client’s living space.

Light meal preparation

Help preparing meals according to established preferences and instructions.

Our service scope: Primetime focuses on non-medical personal assistance. We do not provide skilled nursing, medical treatment or therapy through these services.
