Support built around daily life.

The specific tasks and hours available depend on the client’s program, care needs and service authorization.

Activities of Daily Living

Respectful help with bathing, dressing, grooming, toileting, mobility and other authorized daily routines.

Attendant Care

Dependable personal assistance that supports individual routines, functional needs and approved care schedules.

Respite Care

Temporary support that gives a family caregiver time to rest, work or handle other responsibilities.

Personal Care

Thoughtful assistance with personal hygiene, dressing, grooming and other approved everyday activities.

Non-medical prompts to follow the medication schedule established by the client’s healthcare provider. Not medication administration.

Help with the routines that make home feel like home.

When included in the authorized service plan, everyday assistance may also include:

Companionship

Conversation and considerate support during approved care visits.

Laundry

Help with washing, drying, folding and putting away the client’s clothing.

Light housekeeping

Assistance with approved light household tasks in the client’s living space.

Light meal preparation

Help preparing meals according to established preferences and instructions.

Our service scope: Primetime focuses on non-medical personal assistance. We do not provide skilled nursing, medical treatment or therapy through these services.
