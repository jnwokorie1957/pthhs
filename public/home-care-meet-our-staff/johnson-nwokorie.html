Johnson Nwokorie

Contact PrimetimeMeet Our Team

Leadership rooted in Houston home care

Johnson Nwokorie leads Primetime Home Health Services as Administrator and founder. His professional background combines business administration, finance and decades of practical leadership in the home-care industry.

A graduate of Texas Southern University, he earned undergraduate and graduate business degrees and developed an early interest in improving how home-care organizations serve families. Over his career, he has helped establish home-care businesses in Houston and has focused on building dependable systems for clients, caregivers and office teams.

A long-term vision for Primetime

Johnson’s leadership emphasizes individualized service, operational sustainability and a culture built around integrity, dignity, confidentiality and respect. Primetime’s continued growth reflects a focus on supporting clients and attendants while maintaining a local Houston identity.

His goal is for Primetime to continue improving the quality and consistency of personal assistance services while adapting to changes in healthcare, technology and the needs of the communities the agency serves.

About Primetime Home Health Services

Primetime provides non-medical personal assistance services to eligible children, adults and seniors throughout Greater Houston. The agency works with multiple Texas Medicaid managed-care programs and supports families with authorized attendant care, personal care, respite services and help with activities of daily living.
