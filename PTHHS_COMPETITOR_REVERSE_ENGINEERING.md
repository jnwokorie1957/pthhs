1. **Exact-Match Program Naming**
   - Titles and URLs include "STAR+PLUS", "PHC", "FC", "CAS", "PAS" explicitly
   - PTHHS uses generic "Attendant Care Services", "Respite Care", "Insurance Accepted"

2. **Dedicated Program/Service Pages**
   - Newport has `/star-plus/`, `/services/pas/`, `/services/medicaid-caregivers/`
   - PTHHS has one attendant care page; no dedicated Medicaid/PAS/CAS/Eligibility pages

3. **Eligibility & "How Do I Qualify" Content**
   - Newport, Angels Instead, Total Joy have eligibility guides, step-by-step, assessment criteria
   - PTHHS has no eligibility content; "Insurance Accepted" page is thin and mis-titled

4. **Location-Specific Targeting**
   - CareWorks mentions Houston + Sugar Land, Katy, Pearland, Cypress in one page
   - PTHHS has areas-we-serve page but location pages are thin or duplicate

5. **Review & Trust Signals**
   - CareWorks displays "5.0 stars · 28+ Google reviews" prominently
   - PTHHS has client reviews page but H1 mismatch ("Alzheimer's Care" instead of "Client Reviews")

6. **Educational "What Is" Content**
   - Total Joy has "What Is Personal Assistance Services (PAS) in Houston? Your Complete Guide"
   - PTHHS has no educational content explaining programs, eligibility, or services

7. **MCO/Provider Status Clarity**
   - Competitors explicitly state which MCOs they work with (Superior, Molina, Aetna, UHC)
   - PTHHS does not list accepted managed care plans
