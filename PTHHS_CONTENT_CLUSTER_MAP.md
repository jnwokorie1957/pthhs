# PTHHS Content Cluster Map

**Reviewed:** September 11, 2026  
**Scope:** Non-medical personal assistance services only

| Cluster | Hub | Published spokes | User intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal assistance | `/home-care-services` | Attendant care, personal care, ADLs, medication reminders | What help is available at home? |
| Caregiver support | `/home-care-services/respite-care` | Authorized protective supervision, family-caregiver FAQ, Contact process | How can a household arrange relief or supervision? |
| Texas Medicaid navigation | `/home-care-insurance` | Official Resources, Contact/Get Started, Areas We Serve | Who decides eligibility and authorization, and what does PTHHS do next? |
| Local service questions | `/home-care-areas-we-serve` | 65 city, community, and county routes | Does PTHHS serve this location and how is availability confirmed? |
| Trust and operations | `/home-care-about-us` | Leadership Team, Google Reviews, Careers | Who runs the agency and how can people engage with it? |

The Resources page links to the first three topic hubs. The homepage links to service, eligibility, location, review, and trust hubs. Every location page links to the service details, Areas hub, Contact route, and phone contact. Imported clinical nursing articles remain retired; new spokes require the editorial policy's source and scope review.
