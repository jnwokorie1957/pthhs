# PTHHS Website Ownership and Review Cadence

**Effective:** September 11, 2026

| Area | Accountable role | Operational check | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Service scope and claims | PTHHS Administrator | Confirm all public services remain non-medical PAS and match current operations | Quarterly and before release |
| Owner-reported business metrics | PTHHS Administrator | Reconfirm the awards, customer, satisfaction, attendant and supervisor snapshots in the public-facts register | Quarterly |
| Payer participation | PTHHS Administrator | Reconfirm each displayed plan/program and its publication caveat | Quarterly |
| Service areas | Operations lead | Reconfirm county, region, city, ZIP, payer and staffing availability | Quarterly |
| Reviews | Administrator or communications lead | Recheck public source, attribution, privacy and removal requests | Quarterly |
| Careers | Hiring lead | Confirm openings, role requirements and approved application route | Monthly |
| Health-adjacent content | Administrator or compliance reviewer | Check sources, scope, disclaimer and next review date | Quarterly |
| Links and redirects | Web operations | Crawl internal/external links, redirects, metadata and sitemap | Monthly and after deployment |
| Contact routing | Office operations | Test each published phone/email/form route and document ownership | Monthly and after changes |
| Accessibility | Web operations with human tester | Automated template checks plus keyboard, screen-reader and zoom review | Quarterly and after template changes |
| Performance and security | Web operations | Check budgets, dependencies, caching, headers and browser metrics | Quarterly and after template changes |
| Search visibility | Web operations | Review indexing, canonical selection, queries and landing-page health | Monthly |

Failed checks become release blockers when they could misroute an inquiry, expose private information, misstate service scope, or break a core journey. Other findings receive an owner and target date in `plan.md`.
