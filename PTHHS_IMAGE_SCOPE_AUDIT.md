# Clinical-Looking Image and Caption Audit

**Reviewed:** September 10, 2026  
**Rule:** Public marketing imagery must show non-medical companionship or everyday assistance and must not imply diagnosis, medication administration, clinical monitoring, nursing, wound care, or therapy by PTHHS.

## Actions completed

- Replaced the legacy Contact page, removing its nurse-group hero, “doctress” caption, diagnosis section, and clinical marketing content.
- Replaced both Medication Reminders routes with text-led, non-clinical pages that do not show medication administration.
- Replaced the misleading home-health-agency route with a non-medical PAS explanation and a clear referral distinction for skilled services.
- Replaced the Careers page so imagery/copy no longer presents attendant applicants as clinical home-health professionals.
- Quarantined all imported blog articles and their clinical-looking media from search and normal navigation pending article-level review.
- Retained general caregiver/client imagery on modern pages only where the adjacent copy describes companionship, personal assistance, or activities of daily living.

## Ongoing release rule

New or replacement imagery must be reviewed alongside its filename, alt text, caption, and surrounding copy. Medical equipment, medication handling, scrubs/stethoscopes, examinations, treatment scenes, or clinical charts require written approval and must not be used to market PTHHS's non-medical PAS.
