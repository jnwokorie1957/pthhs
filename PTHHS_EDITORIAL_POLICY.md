# PTHHS Health-Adjacent Editorial Policy

**Effective:** September 11, 2026  
**Owner:** PTHHS Administrator  
**Review cadence:** Quarterly and before any material health-adjacent publication

PTHHS website content must describe non-medical personal assistance services accurately and in plain language. It may explain public program navigation, daily-living support, caregiver support, respite, authorized protective supervision, and non-medical medication reminders. It must not imply that PTHHS diagnoses conditions, administers medication, provides skilled nursing, gives medical advice, delivers therapy, or guarantees eligibility, authorization, outcomes, staffing, or response times.

Health-adjacent educational statements require a current primary government or first-party authoritative source, a source link, a checked date, and review by the PTHHS Administrator or assigned compliance reviewer. PTHHS-specific payer, location, staff, history, review, award, and quantitative claims require owner-approved evidence in the appropriate repository register. Public-program information is not evidence of a PTHHS payer contract.

Every article must identify its intended non-medical purpose, avoid diagnosis-specific advice, direct clinical questions to licensed professionals, and include an emergency limitation where relevant. Review existing content quarterly; update it when authoritative guidance changes, retire it when no longer useful, and keep retired imported articles noindex with permanent redirects until a fresh compliant replacement is approved.
